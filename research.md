## Research Article
This Summary of the article aims to add substance to our lives drawing from various human experiences. This article draws upon goals such as one's general well-being, quality of life, relationships, hobbies, interpersonal values and overall life goals. This article differs from my specific goals, as Zhang, Kaili Chen and Yu, Esther Dawen speak about goals for my much broader perspective rather than specific actionable goals like mine; being going out and buying toys and treats for my dog. This article does share the overall theme that life experiences shape who they are. However is more philosophical and broad.

Zhang, K. C., & Yu, E. D. (2014). Spiritual values and life goals. Asia-Pacific Psychiatry, 6(1), 91–98. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1758-5872.2012.00183.x
